# Dentiscope AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Dentiscope AI (“Dentiscope”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how Dentiscope AI handles information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using Dentiscope AI, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Process

Dentiscope AI is designed to minimize personal data collection.

Users are not required to create an account in order to use the application.

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

* Full name
* Home address
* Phone number
* Government-issued identification
* Personal health records

## 2. Camera, Photo Library, and Scan Images

Dentiscope AI allows users to scan or upload dental instrument images for educational AI-assisted recognition.

When a user chooses to scan with the camera or upload an image from the photo library, the selected image may be transmitted to Dentiscope AI’s own backend server for the sole purpose of generating an educational dental instrument recognition result.

The image may be used to generate:

* Instrument recognition results
* Confidence information
* Similar instrument suggestions
* Educational reference content

Scan images are not used to identify users.

Users should avoid including faces, patient information, documents, or any personally identifiable information in uploaded images.

## 3. AI Processing and Third-Party AI Services

Dentiscope AI does not send user images, photos, or scan data to third-party AI services.

Images selected by the user are processed only through Dentiscope AI’s own backend and AI recognition model for educational dental instrument recognition.

Dentiscope AI does not share user images with third-party AI model providers.

At this time, scan images are not intentionally stored permanently and are not automatically used for dataset collection or machine learning training.

Images are processed temporarily for the requested recognition task and are not retained longer than necessary for that processing, unless the user explicitly consents to a future optional improvement or feedback feature.

## 4. Local Device Storage

Certain application data may be stored locally on your device in order to improve user experience and application functionality, including:

* Favorites
* Scan history
* Premium access status
* App preferences

This information is intended to remain locally stored on your device and is not publicly shared by Dentiscope AI.

## 5. Premium Purchases and Subscriptions

Dentiscope AI may offer premium subscription features through official app marketplaces such as Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Payment transactions are processed securely by the respective platform providers.

Dentiscope AI does not directly collect or store payment card information.

## 6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Dentiscope AI may use privacy-focused analytics and crash reporting services to improve application performance, reliability, and user experience.

These services may collect limited technical information, including:

* Device model
* Operating system version
* App version
* Anonymous usage events
* Crash diagnostics

This information is used solely for improving the application and maintaining service stability.

Analytics and crash reporting data is not used to identify users personally and does not include scan images.

AI-generated predictions may occasionally be inaccurate.

Dentiscope AI does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of AI-generated predictions or educational content.

Users should not rely solely on application results for clinical or professional decisions.
