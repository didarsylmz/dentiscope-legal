# Dentiscope AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Dentiscope AI (“Dentiscope”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how the Dentiscope AI mobile application handles information when you use our services.

By using Dentiscope AI, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Process

Dentiscope AI is designed to minimize personal data collection.

We do not require users to create an account in order to use the application.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

* Full name
* Address
* Phone number
* Government identification
* Personal health records

## 2. Scan Images

When you use the scan feature, images may be temporarily processed to generate AI-based instrument recognition results.

Scan images are not used to identify users.

At this time, scan images are not intentionally stored permanently or used for automated dataset collection.

## 3. Local Device Storage

Certain application data may be stored locally on your device to improve your experience, including:

* Favorites
* Scan history
* Premium access status
* App preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is not intended to be shared publicly.

## 4. Premium Purchases

Dentiscope AI may offer premium subscription features through official app marketplaces such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Payment transactions are processed by the platform providers and not directly by Dentiscope AI.

We do not store your payment card information.

## 5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Dentiscope AI may use privacy-focused analytics and crash reporting tools to improve app stability, performance, and user experience.

These services may collect limited technical information such as:

* Device model
* Operating system version
* App version
* Anonymous usage events
* Crash diagnostics

This information is used solely to improve the application.

## 6. Educational Use Disclaimer

Dentiscope AI is intended for educational and informational purposes only.

The application does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or professional clinical decision-making services.

Users should not rely solely on AI-generated results for clinical decisions.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Dentiscope AI is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be reflected by the “Last updated” date at the top of this document.
